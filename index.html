I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in the MEMOTEF Department at Sapienza University of Rome, where I work under the supervision of Prof. Claudia Ceci as part of the PRIN 2022 project “Stochastic control and games and the role of information.”

I hold a Ph.D. (Doctor Europaeus) in Models for Economics and Finance from Sapienza University of Rome. My dissertation, titled “Optimal Annuitization under Piecewise Deterministic Mortality Force,” was supervised by Prof. Tiziano De Angelis and Prof. Gabriele Stabile, and was awarded cum laude.

I was a Visiting Scholar at Universität Bielefeld (Germany), under the supervision of Prof. Giorgio Ferrari, and at the University of Turin, under the supervision of Prof. Tiziano De Angelis.

My research interests lie in optimal stopping and stochastic optimal control, actuarial and financial mathematics, partial information, and filtering.
