Hi,

I am a Postdoctoral Researcher in Actuarial and Financial Mathematics in the Department of Mathematics at the University of Uppsala, where I work with Erik Ekström.

My research focuses on optimal stopping and free-boundary problems in actuarial and financial mathematics, as well as models under partial information and filtering.

Previously, I was a Postdoctoral Researcher in the ESOMAS Department at the University of Turin, working with Tiziano De Angelis. Prior to that, I held a postdoctoral position in the MEMOTEF Department at Sapienza University of Rome, where I worked with Claudia Ceci.

I hold a Ph.D. in Mathematics for Economic-Financial Applications from Sapienza University of Rome. My thesis was supervised by Tiziano De Angelis and Gabriele Stabile, and was awarded cum laude.
